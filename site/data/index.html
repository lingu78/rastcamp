Rastcamp – open data

The place database used by the Rastcamp app (motorhome stops, campsites, dump stations, water points, rest areas and LPG in Sweden, Norway, Finland and Denmark) is a derivative database of OpenStreetMap, rebuilt monthly from Geofabrik extracts.

  • manifest.json – version, date, counts, SHA-256
  • pois.json.gz – current data (gzip JSON)
  • archive/ – previous versions (pois-YYYY-MM-DD.json.gz)
