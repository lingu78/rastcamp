## Full description (Play + App Store, max 4000)

Where can we empty the tanks? Where can we fill up with water? Where do we sleep tonight?

Rastcamp helps motorhome and caravan travellers in Sweden, Norway, Finland and Denmark find the nearest:

• Motorhome stop (aire / stellplatz)
• Campsite
• Dump station (grey water and cassette)
• Water point
• Rest area
• LPG / bottled gas

WORKS WITHOUT SIGNAL
All places – more than 13,000 across the Nordics – are built into the app. The "Nearby" list, search, place details and your favourites work in airplane mode and deep in the mountains. The background map needs a connection, but the places are always shown.

STRAIGHT TO THE ANSWER
• Tap "Dump station" and instantly see the nearest ones – sorted by distance, with compass direction.
• Tap a place for fee, opening hours, electricity, shower, toilets, water, waste disposal, dogs and contact details – when the information exists.
• "Navigate" opens Apple Maps or Google Maps with directions.
• Save favourites for the trip.
• Search by name.

FREE, NO ACCOUNT, NO ADS
No account, no sign-in, no ads, no in-app purchases. Rastcamp collects no data about you. Your location is only used on your phone to calculate distances.

ALWAYS FRESH DATA
Places come from OpenStreetMap and are updated every month. The app downloads new data automatically when you are online. Spotted a mistake? Tap "Report an error" and fix it in OpenStreetMap – helping every other traveller too.

English, Swedish and Norwegian. Dark mode.
